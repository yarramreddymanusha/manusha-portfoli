## Product Principles

1. **Proof Over Claims:** Let architecture, technical mechanisms, and concrete achievements (patent, hackathon, IIT certification) validate capability rather than generic self-praise.
2. **Zero-Friction Access:** Recruiters and hiring managers should reach key facts (skills, projects, credentials, resume, contact) within seconds without navigation hurdles or broken external dependencies.
3. **Engineered Clarity:** Present system designs, data workflows, and engineering decisions with structured clarity and technical accuracy.
4. **Resilient Simplicity:** Favor zero-dependency, durable primitives (e.g., direct email contact, offline-tolerant interactions) over fragile third-party integrations.
